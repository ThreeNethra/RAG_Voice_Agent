## Decision rights
- Pricing changes: CFO + VP Product, approved by CEO
- Hardware design changes: CTO
- Security exceptions: CISO only
- Hiring above Director level: CEO + Chief People Officer
- Vendor contracts above $250,000/year: CFO and CEO
