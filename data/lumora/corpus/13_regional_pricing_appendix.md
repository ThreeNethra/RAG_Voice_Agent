## Notes
- INR and EUR price lists are reviewed every January based on exchange rates. Rates in a signed contract stay fixed for the contract term.
- EU business customers with a valid VAT ID are invoiced under the VAT reverse-charge mechanism.
- The discount approval rules in the main pricing document apply to regional prices as well.
- Customers in other currencies are quoted in USD.
