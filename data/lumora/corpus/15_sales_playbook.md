# Sales Playbook FY2027 — Internal, Confidential

## Sales motion
- Median sales cycle: 41 days for Growth deals and 94 days for Enterprise deals.
- Account Executive (AE) annual quota: $1.4M in new ARR.
- Commission: 8% of first-year contract value, paid quarterly.
- The deal desk reviews non-standard terms and responds within 1 business day.

## Discount approvals
The authoritative rules are in "Pricing and Plans". As a reminder:
- AEs can approve up to 10% on their own.
- Regional Sales Directors can approve up to 20%.
- Anything above 20% needs VP Sales and CFO sign-off.
- Never promise a discount verbally before the approval is recorded in the CRM.

## Competitor battle cards
| Competitor | Where they win | Where we win |
|---|---|---|
| ThermoSure | Cheapest hardware (about $70 per sensor) | No WHO PQS certification; offline memory of only 7 days |
| ColdWatch | Strong US grocery relationships | Weak EU GDP reporting; no AI risk scoring |
| KelvinGuard | Established in pharma wholesale | Email-only alerting; no webhooks |

Our edge: 90-day offline memory, WHO PQS E006 certification, signed compliance reports.

## Objection handling
- **"You cost more than ThermoSure."** Compare total cost of ownership. Our battery lasts up to 24 months against 12, and calibration is included in the first year.
- **"We already use spreadsheets and data loggers."** Ask what an audit finding costs them and how long audit preparation takes; the MediNord case study cut audit prep from 12 days to 2.
- **"Is the AI safe for regulated shipments?"** Lumora Insights is advisory only and never replaces monitoring.

## Pilots
Free 30-day pilots with up to 20 sensors are standard. Hardware must be returned or purchased at the end of the pilot.
