Standard rate $185/hour; onboarding packages: Bronze $4,500, Silver $12,000, Gold $28,000.

## Discount policy
- Sales reps may approve discounts up to **10%** without approval.
- Regional sales directors may approve up to **20%**.
- Anything above 20% requires VP Sales **and** CFO sign-off.
- Non-profit and vaccine-program customers qualify for a 30% mission discount (requires proof of status).
- The CT-200 migration discount (25% off CT-300) cannot be combined with volume discounts.

## Free trial
30-day pilot with up to 20 sensors; hardware must be returned or purchased at the end of the pilot.

## Payment terms
Net 30 by default. Net 60 requires Finance approval. Late fee: 1.5% per month.
