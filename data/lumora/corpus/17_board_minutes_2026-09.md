# Board of Directors — Meeting Minutes, 24 September 2026
**Attendees:** Ananya Deshpande (Chair), Rohan Iyer, Helena Voss, Arjun Kapoor, Dr. Susan Whitfield. In attendance: Marcus Lindqvist (CFO). | **Classification:** Board Confidential

## 1. Q2 FY2027 results
Marcus presented Q2 FY2027: revenue $38.6M and ARR $128M. The Board noted that full-year guidance had been raised to $150–158M. Helena asked about hardware gross margin (about 44%); Marcus explained the risk around the LTE-M modem second source.

## 2. Decisions
1. **Approved:** a development budget of $6.2M for the CT-400 solar-assist sensor (target Q1 FY2028).
2. **Approved:** the FY2027 hiring plan of 60 net new roles.
3. **Not approved:** the proposal to raise the Starter plan price by 6% from 1 April 2027. The Board asked Product and Finance to return in January 2027 with data on churn sensitivity. The current price list is unchanged.
4. **Approved:** opening a search for a Chief Revenue Officer who would report to the CEO.
5. **Approved:** renewal of the cyber-insurance policy with $15M of coverage.

## 3. Discussion notes (informal)
- Susan asked whether the CT-400 would need a fresh WHO prequalification; Rohan thinks a variation filing will be enough.
- Arjun suggested exploring a Middle East office in FY2028. No decision was taken.
- Helena wants quarterly customer-concentration reporting, since the top 10 customers are 29% of revenue.
