# Lumora Employee Handbook (excerpt, v7.1, effective 2026-04-01)

## 1. Working model
Lumora is **hybrid-first**. Employees are expected in the office **3 days per week (Tue–Thu)** unless their role is designated remote. Fully remote roles are allowed for engineering and customer success with VP approval. Core collaboration hours: 11:00–16:00 in the employee's home office time zone.

## 2. Leave policy
| Leave type | India | Netherlands | USA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Annual / PTO | 24 days | 25 days | 20 days (+ 10 company holidays) |
| Sick leave | 12 days | per statutory | 8 days |
| Parental leave (primary) | 26 weeks | 16 weeks (statutory) + 6 weeks top-up | 16 weeks |
| Parental leave (secondary) | 4 weeks | 5 days statutory + 3 weeks | 6 weeks |
| Bereavement | 7 days | 5 days | 5 days |

- Up to **10 unused PTO days** carry over into the next calendar year; the rest expire on 31 March (India) / 31 December (other regions).
- Leave requests of more than 5 consecutive days require manager approval at least 14 days ahead.
- Unpaid sabbatical: after 5 years of service, up to 8 weeks, with VP and Chief People Officer approval.

## 3. Compensation and review cycle
Performance reviews happen twice a year: **April** (annual review, salary revisions effective 1 May) and **October** (mid-year check-in, no salary changes except promotions). Bands are reviewed every January. Equity refresh grants are considered once a year in the April cycle.

## 4. Expenses and travel
- Meals during business travel: up to $60/day (US), €50/day (EU), ₹2,500/day (India).
- Flights over 6 hours may be booked in premium economy. Business class requires CEO approval.
- Expenses must be submitted within **30 days** via the Expensify portal; older claims are rejected unless Finance approves an exception.
- Any single expense above $500 needs prior manager approval; above $5,000 needs VP approval.
- Home-office stipend: $600 one-time on joining, plus $40/month internet reimbursement for remote employees.

## 5. Learning budget
Each employee has an annual learning budget of **$1,500** (courses, books, conferences). Unused budget does not roll over. Managers approve; Finance reimburses.

## 6. On-call policy (Engineering and Support)
- On-call rotations are one week long, with a minimum of 4 engineers per rotation.
- On-call pay: $350 per week (US/EU) or ₹18,000 per week (India), plus $50/₹3,000 per page handled outside working hours.
- After a night incident of over 2 hours, the engineer receives a half-day off the next day.
- Severity definitions are in the Incident Management Policy.

## 7. Code of conduct (summary)
Zero tolerance for harassment or discrimination. Report concerns to people@lumora.example or the anonymous Ethics line. Retaliation against reporters is grounds for dismissal. Conflicts of interest, gifts above $100, and outside employment must be disclosed to the Chief People Officer.

## 8. Probation and notice
Probation: 6 months (India), 2 months (Netherlands), none (US at-will). Notice period: 60 days (India, senior roles 90), per statute (Netherlands), 2 weeks (US).
