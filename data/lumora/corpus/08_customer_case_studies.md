# Customer Case Studies

## Case Study 1: MediNord Distribution (Poland / pharma)
- **Plan:** Growth, 310 sensors, RouteIQ add-on since January 2026
- **Challenge:** Frequent excursions on cross-border routes from Gdańsk to Prague; audit findings under EU GDP.
- **Solution:** CT-300 sensors on every pallet; Compliance Vault for automated GDP reports; RouteIQ to pick carriers with the best border-dwell history.
- **Results (6 months):** Excursion incidents down 31%; audit prep time cut from 12 days to 2 days; estimated savings €410,000.
- **Note:** MediNord was one of the customers affected by INC-2026-0312 (see postmortem) and received an SLA credit; they renewed for 3 years in July 2026.

## Case Study 2: FreshBasket (India / grocery)
- **Plan:** Enterprise, 4,800 sensors across 210 stores and 340 trucks
- **Challenge:** Dairy and ready-to-eat spoilage of 3.4% across the network.
- **Solution:** Store-level hub H5 gateways, rules tuned per product class, truck sensors for last-mile.
- **Results:** Spoilage down to 1.9% within 9 months; yearly savings about ₹14.2 crore. Integration with SAP completed through Lumora Professional Services (Gold package).
- **Quote from their COO:** "We now know about a failing fridge before the store manager does."

## Case Study 3: Arctic Catch Co. (Norway / seafood export)
- **Plan:** Growth, 520 sensors (slightly over the 500 included — billed at $6/extra sensor)
- **Challenge:** Fresh salmon shipments to Asia lost quality during air-freight transfers in Dubai and Singapore.
- **Solution:** Lumora Insights risk scores for each in-flight shipment; alerts when predicted excursion probability exceeds 40%.
- **Results:** Claim rate fell from 4.8% to 2.6% of shipments over two quarters.

## Case Study 4: VaxBridge Africa (NGO / vaccine programs)
- **Plan:** Enterprise with 30% mission discount; 1,150 CT-300 + 120 CT-Probe P10 units.
- **Challenge:** Last-mile vaccine delivery across rural regions with patchy connectivity.
- **Solution:** CT-300's 90-day offline memory and BLE sync via the mobile app; NB-IoT where available.
- **Results:** 97% data completeness despite connectivity gaps; reported wastage down from 14% to 6%.
- **Funding:** Partially funded by a global health foundation grant.
