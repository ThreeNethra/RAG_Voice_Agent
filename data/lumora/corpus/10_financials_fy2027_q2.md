## Revenue mix (Q2 FY2027)
- Platform subscriptions: $21.0M (54%)
- Hardware: $12.0M (31%)
- AI add-ons: $4.2M (11%)
- Services: $1.4M (4%)

## Customer metrics
- Active customers: 318 (+22 net in the quarter)
- Net revenue retention: 118% (trailing 12 months)
- Average contract value (ACV): $402K
- CAC payback: 19 months
- Sensors in the field: 214,000 CT-class devices

## Hardware economics
Average CT-300 bill-of-materials cost is $41, landed cost $47 including freight and duties. At a $89 list price, blended gross margin on hardware is ~**44%**. Component shortage risk for the LTE-M modem flagged by Operations; second source from Brightline is being qualified.

## FrostLogic acquisition
In January 2026 Lumora acquired FrostLogic (a 14-person Rotterdam startup specializing in ML for shelf-life prediction) for **$9.5M** (60% cash, 40% stock). The team now works within Data Science & AI and contributed to Lumora Insights.

## Guidance
- FY2027 revenue guidance: $150–158M (raised from $145–152M)
- Target to reach operating breakeven by **Q4 FY2028** (Jan–Mar 2028).
- Hiring plan: 60 net new roles in FY2027, concentrated in Engineering and Customer Success.

## Risks flagged
1. Concentration: top 10 customers = 29% of revenue.
2. FX exposure to INR and EUR.
3. Reliance on a single contract manufacturer for ~70% of units.
4. Regulatory changes in EU GDP Annex 15 guidance.

## Note on prior figures
Earlier internal slides quoted Q2 FY2027 revenue as "$37.9M" — that was a pre-close estimate. The figure above ($38.6M) is final.
