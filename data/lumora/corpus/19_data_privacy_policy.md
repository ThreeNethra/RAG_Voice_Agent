# Data Privacy and Customer Data Handling Policy (v3.2)
**Owner:** Data Protection Officer Elena Rossi (Rotterdam), together with the CISO

## Roles
Lumora acts as a data processor for customer sensor data and as a data controller for account and contact data.

## Data subject requests
Requests under GDPR (access, rectification, erasure) are answered within 30 days. They are sent to privacy@lumora.example.

## When a customer contract ends
- Customer sensor data and reports remain available for export for 30 days after termination.
- All customer data is then deleted from production within 90 days of the termination date.
- Encrypted backups that still contain the data are purged within a further 35 days.
- Compliance Vault reports are immutable and cannot be restored later, so customers should export them before termination.

This is different from the retention period on a plan (13 months, 3 years or 7 years), which applies while the contract is active.

## Sub-processors
AWS (hosting), Pigeon API and Zephyr SMS (messaging) and Mailrun (email). Customers are notified 30 days before a new sub-processor is added.

## Personal data in sensor data
Temperature and humidity readings are not personal data. Driver names attached to shipments may be personal data; those fields are optional and can be disabled per account.
