# Lumora Systems, Inc. — Company Overview

**Document owner:** Corporate Communications | **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-15 | **Classification:** Internal + Public

## Who we are
Lumora Systems, Inc. is a B2B technology company that helps food, pharmaceutical, and vaccine supply chains keep temperature-sensitive goods safe from the factory to the final delivery. We build IoT sensors, a cloud monitoring platform, and AI-driven route and risk tools.

- **Founded:** March 2018 in Pune, India, by Ananya Deshpande (CEO) and Rohan Iyer (CTO)
- **Headquarters:** Bengaluru, India (moved from Pune in January 2021)
- **Other offices:** Rotterdam (Netherlands, EMEA hub, opened 2022), Austin (Texas, North America hub, opened 2023), Singapore (APAC sales, opened 2024)
- **Employees:** 412 as of 30 September 2026
- **Customers:** 318 active enterprise customers in 29 countries
- **Ticker / status:** Privately held. Series C closed in June 2025 ($84M led by Meridian Growth Partners).

## Mission and values
**Mission:** "No spoiled dose, no wasted meal." We want zero preventable losses in the cold chain.

Our five values (internally called **LUMEN**):
1. **L**isten first — customer problems before our solutions.
2. **U**nderstand the data — never ship a number we can't explain.
3. **M**ove carefully with safety-critical systems — a false negative can cost lives.
4. **E**xplain simply — write docs a new hire can follow.
5. **N**ever hide a mistake — blameless postmortems, always.

## Key milestones
- 2018: Company founded; first prototype sensor "Ember-1" shipped to 3 pilot customers.
- 2019: ColdTrack v1 launched; first ISO 9001 certification.
- 2020: Pandemic vaccine logistics drives 6x growth; WHO PQS-aligned sensor released.
- 2021: Series A ($12M). HQ moves to Bengaluru.
- 2022: LumoraOS 2.0 and Rotterdam office.
- 2023: Series B ($31M). Austin office. SOC 2 Type II achieved.
- 2024: RouteIQ beta. Singapore office.
- 2025: Series C ($84M). ColdTrack Gen 3 (CT-300) launched. ISO 27001 certified.
- 2026: Lumora Insights GA (April). Acquired small startup FrostLogic (see financials).
